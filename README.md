# Tadcu — Episode 3: "The Vet"

An episode of the **Tadcu** animated cartoon series. Tadcu (Welsh for
"Grandpa"), his sheepdog Bryn, the vet Dilys, a cat with opinions, and one
very contested bill.

## What's in here

| File | Description |
| --- | --- |
| `index.html` | The complete animated episode — self-contained, no dependencies. Open it in any browser. |
| `script.md` | Full script, shot list and storyboard notes. |
| `narration.md` | Timed voice-over recording script. |

## How to watch

Open `index.html` in a browser, press ▶ (or space). Controls: play/pause,
restart, scene skip, click the bar to scrub by scene, keys 1–7 to jump
straight to a scene.

## Story so far

Episodes 1 & 2 are in `../test01` and `../test02`. Episode 2 ended with
Bryn riding the parcel like a county trophy; this one begin with a promise
made at the end: **an appointment neither of them will admit to being
afraid of.**

## Episode 3 summary

The Cwm Twp waiting room holds, today: one cat that hates everybody, one
farm magazine, and a dog doing an impression of a festival statue. Dilys
the vet declares Bryn fit enough to evacuate a field; Tadcu disputes the
forty-pound bill with eggs, tea, and personal Christmas cards; and the
ledger eventually gets settled **in paw**.

## New in this episode

- **Dilys** — the vet of Cwm Twp surgery. Mint scrubs, ponytail, clipboard.
  Dispenses truths at only two speeds: "fit" and "paid".
- **The Patient Zero Cat** — a basket, a blanket, two ears, a temper.
- **The ledger** — where all disputes end, inked appropriately.
