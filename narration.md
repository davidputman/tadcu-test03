# Tadcu — Episode 3: "The Vet"
## Narration (voice-over) script — recording copy

*One narrator, same voice as Episodes 1–2: warm, unhurried, amused. The
waiting room reads like an airport lounge; the bill reads like an obituary.
Music bed: soft harp with wheezing accordion for the vet's quarters.*

Character lines stay as on-screen subtitle bubbles and are shown in *italics*
for the editor only. **BOLD = narrator, to be recorded.**

## Recording notes

- **Runtime:** ~82 seconds; ~8 narration lines, ≈170 words.
- **The exam scene (0:19–0:34)** wants mock-solemn delivery — the vet
  as matron, Bryn as an England trialist.
- **The pause in "The Bill"** (0:44) is the episode's biggest laugh;
  hold it, then hit the paw print.
- **"BRYN BEATS THE NEEDLE"** read like a radio sports headline.
