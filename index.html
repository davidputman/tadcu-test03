Narrator At the vet’s, for once, the queue was small: one cat that hated everybody — and us.
Tadcu Reminders, Bryn: wag if it hurts, sit brave if it doesn’t — and STOP eyeing my thermos.
Bryn (stone-faced. professional.)
THE CAT (hisses at BOTH of them)
Narrator In she came: Dilys — vet, dispenser of truths, bringer of needles.
Dilys Heart of a lion, this one. Fit — unless the tail’s a problem, and tails don’t.
Tadcu Thirty-four kilograms?! He was thirty-three last week. Someone has found the biscuit tin.
Narrator Bryn walked out mid-examination. Confidence, is what that’s called.
Narrator The bill, said Dilys, was forty pounds.
Tadcu Or — hear me out — tea for two, twelve eggs, and my personal thank-you.
Dilys (a silence with an outline)
Narrator Silence, then. The loudest kind.
Dilys Paw print. On the ledger. NOW.
Narrator For the record: settled, in paw.
Tadcu Diolch, Dilys! And no biscuits till Monday, mind.
Narrator And the County Herald headline wrote itself: BRYN BEATS THE NEEDLE.
