### SCENE 1 — THE WAITING ROOM (0:05.5–0:19.5)

**INT. Cwm Dwl SURGERY — MORNING**

Teal waiting room. Three chairs. A cat in a basket (red blanket, two ears,
one grudge). Bryn sits like a statue in most of the middle chair, watching
Tadcu read *Goat Weekly*.

- **NARRATOR:** "At the vet's, for once, the queue was small — a cat that
  hated everybody, and us."
- **TADCU:** "Reminders, Bryn: wag if it hurts, sit brave if it doesn't
  — and STOP eyeing my thermos."
- **BRYN:** *(stone-faced. professional.)*
- **NARRATOR:** "The cat hissed. Some patients simply have no bedside
  manner."

### SCENE 2 — THE EXAMINATION (0:19.5–0:34.5)

**INT. THE EXAM ROOM**

Bryn on the gurney, standing at attention as though measurement alone is
an insult.

- **NARRATOR:** "In she came: Dilys — vet, dispenser of truths, bringer
  of needles."
- **DILYS:** "Heart of a lion, this one. Fit — unless the tail's a
  problem, and tails don't."
- **TADCU:** "Thirty-four kilograms?! He was thirty-three last week.
  Someone has found the biscuit tin."
- **NARRATOR:** "Bryn walked out mid-examination. Confidence, is what
  that's called."

Gags:
1. The stethoscope listens to the tail wag and reports it as a heartbeat.
2. Bryn's ears at attention too far.
3. Mid-exam exit, tail high, like a man leaving his own party.

### SCENE 3 — THE BILL (0:34.5–0:48.5)

**INT. THE FRONT DESK**

- **NARRATOR:** "The bill, said Dilys, was forty pounds."
- **TADCU:** "Or — hear me out — tea for two, twelve eggs, and my
  personal thank-you."
- **DILYS:** *(deadpan)...*
- **NARRATOR:** "Silence, then. The loudest kind."
- **DILYS:** "Paw print. On the ledger. NOW."

### SCENE 4 — THE PARADE (0:48.5–1:01.5)

**EXT. THE ROAD HOME — EVENING**

Bryn rides the thermos cushion like the furthest back of a county
triumph; sheep line each bank, hopping the wire-fence banking (hop-high),
clapping with their hooves (they don't have hands).

- **NARRATOR:** "For the record: settled, in paw."
- **TADCU:** "Diolch, Dilys. And no biscuits till Monday, mind."
- **NARRATOR:** "And the County Herald headline wrote itself: BRYN BEATS
  THE NEEDLE."
- **DILYS:** "NEXT!"

**THE END.**
